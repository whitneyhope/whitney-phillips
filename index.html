About

I have a habit of looking at systems and asking, why are we doing things this way?

I'm a high school English educator, Speech & Debate coach, and program builder interested in the intersection of education policy, rhetoric, program design, and implementation.

My work with students centers on evidence evaluation, argumentation, rhetorical analysis, communication, and perspective-taking. It has also made me increasingly interested in the systems surrounding those experiences: who has access, how programs are designed, what implementation requires, and whether outcomes actually match the intent.
